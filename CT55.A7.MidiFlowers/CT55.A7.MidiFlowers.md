* **▶ Tour** in the Movie group of the bar (key **T**; X-TOUCH encoder 8 push; Launchpad top-row User button; nanoKEY2

  `?w=1280&h=720` a fixed movie size (the picture is scaled to the window).
* The fade and the title are drawn in WebGL over the picture (`draw()`, an orthographic overlay), so they are in the movie.

## The bunching — what caused it and the fix

Measured in A3 (landed flowers after 8 s): with Swirl flowers on and standing still, **up to 54 %** of the flowers lay
within 1 m of the back wall; travelling, ~17 %. Three causes:
1. **The vortex travels with the emitter**, and on its trailing side its flow pointed at the back wall, so every
   flower left behind was swept there. A4: while travelling the vortex turns the other way (its trailing flow leads
   out into the room) and fades out behind the emitter; standing still it eases back (`W.vsense`).
2. **The air blew through the wall.** A4 uses the *method of images* — a mirror vortex behind the wall (opposite turn,
   same inflow), so next to the wall the air runs along it, never into it; the turbulence's flow into the wall fades
   out within 1.5 m and a slight draught leads away from it.
3. **Flowers were held only 6 cm from the wall face**, so their petals went into it ("under the wall"). A4: the whole
   flower stays in front (`wallZ + flower radius`), an air cushion slows flowers heading at the wall, they bounce off it,
   the gush throws less toward the wall, and the emitter's line is further out (3.7–5.2 m).
Result (test, every scene, opening state): **< 5 % within 1 m of the wall, none in or under it** (measured 0–2 %).

  ± button get the matching value control. Saved in the browser (`localStorage` key `CT55.midimap.learned`), shown in
  yellow in the MIDI panel, overriding the file; *Forget learned MIDI* clears them. Works for unknown devices (CT45 generic).
* The small grey text under each on-screen button names its MIDI control on the connected devices (e.g. `XT top2 · LP r1c2`).

## The movie (every CT54 movie problem, solved the way CT54.A6 solved it)

| CT54 problem | CT55 |
|---|---|
| ffmpeg / server / batch file failed on Windows | none: WebCodecs in the page → MP4 (mp4-muxer) → downloaded to your Downloads folder |
| movie at the wrong (fast) speed | constant frame rate, frame i = i/fps; while recording each drawn frame advances the scene **exactly** 1/fps, so the movie plays at the speed the screen shows when not recording (the screen itself may slow while recording; that does not reach the movie) — tested |
| low resolution / soft | the picture's real device-pixel size (window × devicePixelRatio, even, ≤ 3840×2160) is the movie size; constant-quality software H.264 first (`?q=18`), then high-bit-rate H.264 profiles, then VP9; each must encode a test frame first |
| no audio | the **same** software synth plays the speakers and the movie: each frame renders exactly 48000/fps samples → AAC (else Opus), locked to the picture; while recording, you hear those same samples |
| page blank / waiting on the recorder | recorder prepared in the background with time limits; size changes re-check it |
| buttons / text in the movie | the movie is the canvas only; buttons, HUD, help, toasts are page elements |

* 60 fps by default (smooth falling flowers); 30 fps in the MIDI panel or `?fps=30`. The window size is locked during a
  take (the picture is CSS-scaled if you resize).
* The HUD line shows the encoder, size, fps and the actual Mbit/s while recording.
* URL options: `?q=` quality (lower = sharper), `?mbps=`, `?encoder=hw`, `?fps=30`, `?shadow=4096` (shadow map size;
  default 2048), `?max=` flower count (default 9000), `?midi=0` (don't ask for MIDI at start).

## Sound (`_sound.js`)

Pastel bells in C-major pentatonic for flowers (at most ~7 a second, panned to where the emitter is on screen); the pulse =
a 3-note bell chord + a soft low thump; swirling flowers make a band-passed wind (louder with more flowers and strength;
a little with travel speed). Not a microphone: "video with audio" = the app's own sound (ask in A2 if a mic was meant).

## Design notes

* Flower state is structure-of-arrays (`F.*`), states 0 empty / 1 air / 2 landed / 3 swirling; three ring buffers (one
  per kind) recycle the oldest flower when 9000 are out. Air: gravity, drag 0.9/s up, 6.5/s down (≈1.5 m/s fall),
  sinusoidal sway + matching tilt. Swirl: velocity eased toward tangential ω·r with ω = 1.9·strength/(1+0.22 r), plus
  pull to each flower's own radius (0.7–4.5 m) and height (0.35–3.75 m), plus the travel velocity, centred on the
  emitter's track point.
* Floor 640 m plane that jumps in whole 4 m tiles under the view (pattern stays put); shadow box and sun follow the view.
* `NeutralToneMapping`, sRGB output; flowers get 20 % of their own colour as emission (bright pastel under shadow).
* All actions go through `CT55.act(name, phase)`; value controls through `CT55.setValue(name, {delta}|{value})`.
